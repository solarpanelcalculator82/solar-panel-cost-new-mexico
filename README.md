## What to Compare Before You Buy

- Get quotes from multiple licensed installers
- Ask about panel, inverter and workmanship warranties
- Confirm roof condition before installation
- Review your utility's net metering or export-rate policy
- Compare cash, loan and lease/PPA options carefully
